# Appearance and Stickers

Rezure is a tool you leave open all day, so it lets you make it yours. Everything here is purely
visual and applies straight away.

## Light and dark

The **Light** / **Dark** button in the title bar switches modes. It's greyed out on the
**Sky Glass** theme, which always uses light text on a dark backdrop.

## Themes

The **Appearance** page ("Theme, decoration and how the window looks. Changes apply right away.")
shows each theme as a live miniature of the app. Filter them with **All**, **Default**,
**Girls**, and **Mens**.

| Group | Theme | Look |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Default | **Rezure** | Coral red over a warm glass backdrop (the default) |
| Default | **Full Glass** | Crystal-clear glass over an iridescent backdrop |
| Default | **Clear Glass** | Colourless glass on white — pure light and edges |
| Default | **Sky Glass** | Frosted, sky-tinted panels over a dusk sky; always white text |
| Girls | **Blossom** | Pink and lavender, scattered with petals |
| Girls | **Soft Pink** | Clear, airy glass over soft pink, with bubbles |
| Girls | **Lavender Dream** | Lilac and baby blue, with a crescent moon and stars |
| Girls | **Peach** | Warm peach and cream, with little hearts |
| Girls | **Matcha** | Soft mint and sage, with tiny leaves |
| Mens | **Midnight** | Navy and teal with a faint grid |
| Mens | **Navy** | Deep navy with a touch of gold and soft waves |
| Mens | **Carbon** | Graphite and orange, with a carbon-fibre weave |
| Mens | **Forest** | Olive and deep green, with map contour lines |
| Mens | **Terminal** | Neon green on black, with CRT scanlines |

**Show background decoration** (on by default) turns the theme's pattern behind the glass on or
off. The theme's colours stay either way.

## Adjustments

"Fine-tune any theme." These work on top of whichever theme you pick.

| Control | Range | Default | Use it when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Brightness** | 70–120% | 100% | The whole window feels too bright or too dim |
| **Colour intensity** | 50–150% | 100% | You want the theme more muted or more vivid |
| **Glass opacity** | 0–60% | 0% (as designed) | Text is hard to read over a busy backdrop — more solid panels help |
| **Interface size** | 90, 100, 110, 125% | 100% | Text and controls are too small or too big — like zooming the window |
| **Reduce motion** | on/off | Off | You'd rather have no transitions or animations |

**Reset to default** puts the adjustments back. It doesn't change your theme, decoration, or
light/dark choice.

## Stickers

The **Decorations** page lets you stick stickers anywhere on Rezure's window. They're purely
cosmetic: they appear on every page except Decorations itself, and they never get in the way of a
click.

### Place a sticker

1. Click a sticker in the **Stickers** palette ("Click one to add it."). It's added to the
   preview — a miniature of the window.
2. Drag it into place. **Arrow keys** nudge it (hold **Shift** for bigger steps); **Delete**
   removes it.
3. With a sticker selected, set its **Size** (2–25% of the window's width) and **Rotation**, or
   use **Flip**, **Duplicate**, **Bring to front**, and **Remove**.

The palette has 24 built-in stickers in two groups, **Girls** (bow, hearts, sakura, kitty,
butterfly, rainbow, and more) and **Mens** (gamepad, rocket, coffee, terminal, robot, planet, and
more), plus **Downloaded** for ones you've added from the catalog.

You can place up to **40** stickers. The counter shows how many are in use. **Show stickers**
hides them all without removing them, and **Remove all** clears the window (click it twice to
confirm).

### Browse the catalog

**Browse** opens more stickers from Rezure's online catalog. Search, or filter by category, and
click **Download** — a downloaded sticker joins your palette and keeps working offline. **Update**
appears when the catalog has a newer image for one you have; **Remove** deletes it (and, after a
second click, any copies placed on the window).

Every download is checked before it's kept: it must be an SVG, PNG, or WebP of at most 256 KB,
match the catalog's checksum, and — for SVGs — contain no scripts or other active content.
Anything that fails isn't saved. You can keep up to 100 downloaded stickers, in
`C:\rezure\stickers`.

Offline, the catalog shows what it loaded last time ("Can't reach the sticker catalog. Showing
what was loaded last time.") with **Try again**. The catalog request carries no device ID.
